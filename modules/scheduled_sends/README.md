## Scheduled Sends

This module allows users to compose emails and schedule them for delivery at a future date and time. Rather than sending immediately, users can prepare messages in advance and have them delivered automatically when the scheduled time arrives.

The module queues outgoing messages with their associated delivery times and handles actual transmission through background processing. It integrates with Cypht's mail composition and SMTP infrastructure, providing users with a dedicated management interface for viewing, editing, or canceling scheduled messages before they are sent.

The smtp module should be enabled to use this one.
